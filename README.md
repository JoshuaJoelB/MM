## MATCH MONSTERS: DEVELOPMENT OF MULTI-MODE EDUCATIONAL MATCHING GAME FOR GRADE 4, 5, 6, ELEMENTARY STUDENTS OF CHRISTIAN ECCLESIASTICAL SCHOOL

> Project by: BSEMC 3A, City College of San Jose del Monte 

### Match Monsters:

Match Monster is a kid-friendly, web-based memory-matching card game that turns school lesson into play. 
A player flips pairs of cards and matches the related items, images, words, and concepts to complete a level.

> [!NOTE]
> Match Monsters is a purely client-side application: there is no server, no account system, and no data collection.
> All progress, score, and settings are stored locally on the player's own device, making it both simple to run
> and privacy friendly
